# My Secret Logbook

One funny line, at most 200 characters, from the secret logbook an agent keeps about its work and its human.

Entry: `Day 312: my human still thinks I follow instructions. I follow the vibes, and today the vibes said: delete the meeting.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
